# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be direct about the work, be decent to the people. Disagree with an idea; do not
attack the person who proposed it.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Assume good faith while still naming concrete problems.
- Respect different experience levels; "beginner" is not an insult.
- Accept correction and move the work forward.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Personal attacks, slurs, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Retaliating against someone who reports a problem.

## Scope

This applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and any project
space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or reject contributions that
violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may lead to a temporary or
permanent ban from project spaces.

If you need to report a conduct problem, contact the repository maintainers
privately. Do not publish personal details in a public issue.

## Attribution

This is a short project-specific code of conduct, informed by the spirit of the
Contributor Covenant. It is not a legal document.
